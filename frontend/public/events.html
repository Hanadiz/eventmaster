The Boy Who Planted Laughter

📅 One Day, When I Was Ten

A true-ish story, stretched just a little — to show you that every ordinary day is a classroom.

☀️ Morning
Wake Up With Purpose. Mama said, "The day has already started its journey. Don't let it travel without you." I jumped up — not from fear, but because waking up felt like an invitation.
🪣 Mid-Morning
Work Sings If You Let It. Fetching water from the borehole, I sang terribly, making lizards run. I finished faster and arrived home with a wet smile.
🍛 Afternoon
Sharing Is Seasoning. One bowl of posho and beans, shared among three. Baba said, "A meal eaten alone fills the stomach but empties the heart." We invented stories about the beans — it was the best meal of my life.
⚽ Late Afternoon
Losing Is a Teacher in Disguise. My team lost. I sulked, but an older boy told me to congratulate the winners. Shaking their hands made me stronger, not weaker.
🌅 Evening
Family Is the Real Wealth. Sitting on the veranda, no phones, just the sky and Mama's stories. Baba strummed the adungu. I felt belonging.
🌙 Night
Sleep Is an Act of Faith. I whispered, "What if tomorrow is worse?" Mama said, "What if it's better? Sleep well." I trusted the night.
