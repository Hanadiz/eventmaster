About EventMaster Uganda

We're on a mission to transform how Uganda discovers and experiences live events.

Our Story

Founded in 2024, EventMaster Uganda was born out of a simple frustration: finding and booking tickets for events in Uganda was too complicated. From sold‑out concerts at the Kampala Serena to local workshops in Gulu, we saw a need for a platform that makes event discovery and ticketing seamless.

Today, we serve thousands of event‑goers and hundreds of organizers across all four regions of Uganda. Our platform supports everything from small community gatherings to major international conferences, all with the convenience of mobile money payments and instant digital tickets.
